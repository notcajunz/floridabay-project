Questions

Good to know.

Where can I join the Florida Bay Discord?

Use our official invite at discord.gg/floridabay. Join the server, verify your account, and keep Discord open when you connect so your roles sync automatically.

How do I connect to the server?

Open FiveM, press F8, and paste connect join.floridabay.net. The Play button on this page does the same thing automatically.

What is a Florida Bay case ID?

Every ban and blacklist - whether it came from Discord, the network, or in-game - gets one stable random ID like FB-482731. It shows up in your DMs, the staff logs, and the member portal, so everyone is talking about the same record.

How do I appeal a ban?

Sign in to the member portal with Discord, open Punishments, and choose Appeal next to the case. Staff review it in a private ticket, and an approved appeal lifts the ban automatically.

How do I get help from staff?

Open a ticket from the support panel in Discord or from the portal. Both create the same private channel, and you can follow its status on the website.
