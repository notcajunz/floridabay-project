      Driving with CarPlay

      The cursor never locks your controls – you can steer, brake and shift while you tap the screen.

      Press F9 or ESC to hide the cursor and shrink CarPlay to a small player. Press the key again to bring the full screen back.

      Use /carplay to close it completely. Typing in a text box pauses vehicle controls so letters don't drive the car.
