Drop your song files in this folder (.mp4 or .webm, keep them ~20 MB or less).
Then add a line to Config.Tracks in assets/scripts/config.js, for example:

  { title: "Janice STFU", artist: "Drake", file: "assets/media/music/Drake-JaniceSTFU.webm", poster: "assets/media/posters/janice.jpg", length: "3:56" },

Pictures for the player go in assets/media/posters/ (a missing picture is just left blank).
The song plays in the background while the beach scene stays on screen.
